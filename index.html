Privacy Policy — Pixvera Pro: Image & PDF Studio

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Pixvera Pro ("the App", "we", "our") is developed and maintained to help you scan documents, edit photos, and create PDFs. Your privacy matters to us, and this app is designed to work entirely on your device.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

Pixvera Pro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of the following:

  • Personal identifying information (name, email, phone number)
  • Scanned documents, photos, or PDFs you create
  • OCR (text recognition) results
  • Signatures or stamps you draw
  • Location data or GPS metadata
  • Analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage tracking data

2. How the App Works

All core features — document scanning, OCR text recognition, photo editing, PDF creation, and digital signatures — are processed locally on your device using Apple's native frameworks (VisionKit, Vision, PencilKit, PDFKit, Core Image). Nothing is uploaded to any server owned by us or any third party.

3. Permissions We Request

Permission Why we need it
Camera To scan physical documents using the built-in Document Scanner (edge detection).
Photo Library (Picker) To let you choose existing photos to edit or convert to PDF. The app uses Apple's Photos Picker, which does not grant us access to your full photo library — only the specific photo(s) you select.

You can revoke camera access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

4. Data Storage

Files you create (scans, edited photos, signatures, PDFs) are stored only in the app's local storage on your device (your "Vault"). We have no access to these files. Deleting the app or removing an item from your Vault permanently deletes that file from your device.

5. Sharing Your Files

When you tap "Share" or "Print," the app uses Apple's native Share Sheet / AirPrint. Any sharing action (e.g., sending via Messages, Mail, or saving to Files/iCloud) is initiated by you and governed by the privacy policy of the destination app/service you choose — not by Pixvera Pro.

6. Children's Privacy

Pixvera Pro does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no data at all.

7. Third-Party Services

Pixvera Pro does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking services.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: pixveraofficial@gmail.com
